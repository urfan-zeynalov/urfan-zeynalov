### About

I learn by building things, breaking them, and working out why they broke. That curiosity
pulls me in a lot of directions, but it keeps landing on the same place: the point where
hardware and software meet.

- **Power electronics:** MOSFET switching, gate drivers, PWM, DC-DC converters, inverters
- **Embedded systems:** microcontroller firmware in C/C++, moving from the Arduino Uno R3 to bare-metal STM32
- **Where they meet:** motor control, meaning the firmware that drives the power stage that spins the motor
- **Long-term goal:** inverters and hybrid systems for electric vehicles and motorsport powertrains

**Open to:** internships in power electronics, embedded or EV · project collaborations · hackathons and engineering competitions

**Languages:** Azerbaijani (native) · English · Russian · Turkish
